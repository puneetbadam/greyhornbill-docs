# Business Context

**Grey Hornbill** ([greyhornbill.com](https://greyhornbill.com)) is a multi-tenant SaaS golf course booking platform, built and maintained by **Plus Bytes** ([plusbytes.com](https://plusbytes.com)).

Plus Bytes is the parent company / umbrella; Grey Hornbill is its flagship product. The platform is demonstrated to prospective course operators by running **Grey Hornbill Golf Club** live on the platform itself — a real, working dogfood environment rather than a static demo.
