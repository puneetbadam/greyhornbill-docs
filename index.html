Grey Hornbill

Golf course booking, without the phone tag.

Visit greyhornbill.com Built by Plus Bytes

What it is

Grey Hornbill is a booking platform for golf courses. It replaces scattered phone bookings and clunky third-party widgets with one system that keeps every course's tee sheet accurate, in real time, no matter how the booking comes in.

How golfers can book

  • By phone, with George — an AI voice agent that answers, checks live availability, and books the tee time on the call, including group bookings.
  • Online — a booking widget a course can embed directly on its own website.
  • Through the pro shop — staff manage the same tee sheet from a dashboard, with the same live availability golfers see.

All three stay in sync automatically — there's no separate system to reconcile.

Built for real course operations

  • Handles large group bookings, not just single foursomes
  • Respects each course's own hours, tee intervals, and capacity rules
  • Course closures and maintenance windows are handled without manual cleanup
  • Each course gets its own configuration — this is a multi-course platform, not a one-off build

Who it's for

Golf courses and clubs that want fewer missed calls, fewer double-bookings, and a booking experience that feels as good as a great pro shop — without adding staff.
